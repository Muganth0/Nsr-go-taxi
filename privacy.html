Privacy Policy

This page explains the information you may share when contacting NSR GO TAXI through this website.

Information you provide

When you submit an enquiry or booking request, you may choose to share your name, mobile number, pickup and destination details, travel date, passenger count and vehicle preference.

How information is used

Information shared through the website is placed into a WhatsApp message addressed to NSR GO TAXI so the team can respond to your enquiry and confirm availability or pricing. The website's fare calculator processes route inputs in your browser and requests geocoding and route information from OpenStreetMap services.

Third-party services

WhatsApp, OpenStreetMap Nominatim and OSRM are third-party services with their own privacy terms. When you use those features, information may be processed by the relevant provider.

Data retention and contact

This static website does not provide a customer account or online payment facility. For questions about information you have sent to NSR GO TAXI, contact nsrgotaxi@gmail.com or call 7010-123-465.

Updates

This policy may be updated when website features or business practices change. Please review this page periodically.
