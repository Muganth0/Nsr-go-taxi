What Is a One-Way Drop Taxi?

A drop taxi, one-way taxi or one-drop cab is booked to take passengers from one pickup point to one destination. It is useful when you are travelling out of Chennai and do not need the cab for a return journey. Share your exact addresses so the team can check the route and availability.

One-Way Drop Taxi from Chennai

Request a Chennai drop taxi for family visits, business travel, college trips, functions, medical appointments and personal journeys. Select a suitable vehicle based on the number of passengers and luggage. All bookings are subject to vehicle and driver availability.

How to Book a Drop Taxi in Chennai

Enter your pickup location, destination, travel date and time in the booking form. Choose your passenger count and preferred vehicle, then submit your enquiry. You can also call or WhatsApp NSR GO TAXI with the same details. Please mention extra stops, child seats or special luggage needs when enquiring.

Drop Taxi Fare and Additional Charges

Drop taxi fares are quoted according to the route, vehicle category, date and trip requirements. Do not rely on a generic per-kilometre estimate as the final price. Toll and parking charges are payable separately where applicable unless they are specifically included in the confirmed quote.
