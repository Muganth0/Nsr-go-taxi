Useful for Hospital and Family Visits

Vellore is a frequent destination for families travelling from Chennai for medical appointments and other personal needs. A direct taxi can simplify the journey when you need door-to-door travel.

One-Way or Return Travel

Request a one-way drop if you have your onward arrangements in Vellore. For an appointment or short stay, provide the expected return date so a round-trip option can be considered.

Door-to-Door Pickup and Drop

Give the Chennai pickup area and the Vellore destination clearly, especially when the destination is a hospital, hotel, campus or residential address. This avoids ambiguity at booking time.

Plan for Passengers and Luggage

Families travelling for longer stays may have more luggage than a normal city ride. Mention passenger count and luggage so the appropriate available vehicle can be discussed.

What to Include in Your Request

Send pickup location, destination, appointment or travel date, passenger count, luggage needs and one-way/return preference. Final fare and applicable tolls are confirmed before travel.
