Terms & Conditions

Please read these booking terms before sending a taxi enquiry.

Enquiry versus confirmed booking

Submitting a website form or opening a WhatsApp message is a booking enquiry, not a confirmed reservation. A trip is confirmed only after NSR GO TAXI responds and confirms vehicle availability, pickup details and final pricing.

Fare estimates

Website fares are estimates based on the displayed vehicle rates, route distance and selected extras. Final pricing is subject to confirmation by NSR GO TAXI. Local rental packages and Tempo Traveller journeys require a separate quote.

Additional charges

Toll, parking and other trip-specific charges are not included unless explicitly stated in the confirmed quote. Driver allowance and selected extras are shown separately in the estimate where applicable.

Travel details

Customers should provide accurate pickup, destination, date, time, passenger and contact details. Changes to the itinerary may affect vehicle availability and the final fare.

Contact

For booking questions, call 7010-123-465 or email nsrgotaxi@gmail.com.
